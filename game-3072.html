How to Play 3072

1
Swipe or use arrow keys to slide all tiles in one direction.
2
When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one with their combined value. 3 + 3 = 6, 6 + 6 = 12, and so on!
3
After every move, a new tile (3 or occasionally 6) appears in a random empty spot.
4
Reach the 3072 tile to win! Keep going to beat your high score.
5
The sequence goes: 3 → 6 → 12 → 24 → 48 → 96 → 192 → 384 → 768 → 1536 → 3072
6
Game ends when the board is full and no merges are possible.
