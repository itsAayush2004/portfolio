    Engineer · two live platforms

    ARTHIS.space and ARTHIS.land

    ARTHIS.space: 105 games in one swipeable feed

    A mini-games platform with a TikTok-style vertical feed. Open it and you are already playing; swipe up for the next game. 61 single-player games and 44 for friends on one device.

    • Each card in the feed is a complete game, loaded on demand
    • AI agents generate gameplay logic and 3D web content from prompts through MCP tools
    • PostgreSQL backend scaled from 1 to 10,000 daily active users
    • Every game has its own icon, three cover posters and a written guide page
    • One UI that reshapes itself for phone, tablet and desktop, with a lite mode for low-end phones, Data Saver and 2G

    ARTHIS.land: The Wall

    A handcrafted vertical city in the browser. Unity, shipped to WebGL, where every brick on the wall is a room someone built, and 2–4 people explore a space together in real time.

    • Host a room and invite friends with a 4-digit code
    • Each room is a folder holding a GLB build, a thumbnail and a room.json; the city lays itself out from them
    • arthisland-relay: the multiplayer backend. TCP and WebSocket on one port, handshake and framing written from scratch, Python standard library only, Docker-ready
    • Arthis Land Maker docs: an 11-chapter design spec for a Unity editor tool that generates islands, lays out rooms and places furniture

    Engineer · Unity engine

    HexaBed

    A procedural terrain system on a hexagonal grid, and the custom editor tooling that lets a designer build a world without opening a script. The island on the table in the room is generated the same way, live.

    • Generates 160+ hex tiles into a single playable world with varied heights
    • A road generator picks straight, turn, ramp or pit pieces from neighbouring tile heights
    • HexTileEditor: a custom Unity inspector for visual world building, with pivot compensation, 60° rotation snapping and bounds-based prefab alignment
    • GLB export pipeline, 25+ generated world meshes

    Also on the engine bench

    • HexGrid Architect: a 3D hex builder in the browser. Select ground tiles, press Build, then stack houses, towers and roofs. React Three Fiber with custom GLB pieces
    • ARTHIS mini-games app: the feed as an Android app, built with Expo and React Native. Reanimated motion, Skia graphics and a strict 60fps rule

    Both halves · tools and side projects

    The Board: small products, shipped end to end

    CV Forge

    Keep one memory of your whole career, paste any job post, and get a tailored, ATS-proof CV with a score, the gaps and the fixes. It works offline with no account, and it never invents a skill, title, date or number: missing keywords are shown as gaps instead.

    • Reads a job post into requirements, responsibilities and nice-to-haves, and weights about 400 known skills by where they appear
    • Tested on 25 real job posts from October 2026 against hand-made CVs, with every bug the benchmark found kept as a regression test
    • Exports text-based PDF and Word (.docx) in clean reading order

    More on the board

    • Hustiq: a video editor you drive by chatting. Timeline, captions, clip effects and an audio waveform, with FFmpeg running in the browser for export
    • Avatar Maker: design an avatar from presets or mix your own parts, saved to your account
    • 3D asset viewer: a Three.js viewer for ARTHIS furniture and props, with info panels and a download page
    • Daily Rest Flow: a Pomodoro timer with focus, streak and task stats, packaged for Windows with Electron
    • Travel Atlas: a Three.js globe with a parchment texture charting 30+ places I've been and the ones I still dream of
    • Earlier portfolios: the walk-through gallery and the corkboard, now merged into this room
